A LITTLE ABOUT ME

“To dance is to be out of yourself. Larger, more beautiful, more powerful.”

Hi, I'm Breetta! I'm a student who loves exploring new ideas, expressing myself through dance, and getting lost in the pages of a good book.

Dance gives me the freedom to express what words cannot, while reading opens doors to new worlds, perspectives, and possibilities. I'm always learning, growing, and discovering new ways to express who I am.
